Privacy Policy for Hayya

Last updated: September 2026

Hayya is a prayer and Qibla application designed to provide prayer times, Qibla direction, and prayer reminders.

Location Data

Hayya may request access to your device location in order to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction based on your current location.

Location data is used only for these features and is not sold or shared with third parties.

Notifications

Hayya may request permission to send local notifications for prayer reminders. Notification permissions can be changed at any time from your device settings.

Data Storage

App preferences such as language, theme, prayer settings, and favorites may be stored locally on your device.

Data Sharing

Hayya does not sell personal data and does not intentionally share personal data with third parties.

Children

Hayya does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
